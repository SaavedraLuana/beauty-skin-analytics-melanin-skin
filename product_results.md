These are personal observations from testing two products on melanin-rich skin. This is qualitative, not clinical data. Results reflect my experience and may vary for others.

- Product: Mixa cream containing niacinamide (check label for concentration).

## Eucerin Oil Control SPF 50 (oil-free)
- Product: Eucerin Oil Control (oil-free) SPF 50 (broad-spectrum).
- Usage: applied each morning as the last step in routine; reapplied when needed for sun exposure.
- Observations:
  - Finish: provided a mattifying effect suitable for oily/combination skin; minimal shine through the day.
  - Makeup: sits well under makeup with little pilling.
  - White-cast: negligible on my medium/darker complexion (test on a small area first).
  - Protection: felt comfortable for daily outdoor exposure; follow product instructions for reapplication.
- Notes:
  - Non-comedogenic for me, but patch-test if prone to clogging.
  - Consider pairing with antioxidant/niacinamide products at other times of day for combined benefits.
